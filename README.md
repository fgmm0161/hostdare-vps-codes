## **What is HostDare and Why Does It Have a Following?**

HostDare is a small-to-mid-sized VPS provider with data centers in Los Angeles (USA), Osaka and Tokyo (Japan), and Sofia (Bulgaria). What made them stand out early on was offering access to China Telecom's CN2 GIA network at prices that larger providers charge significantly more for.

The CN2 GIA network (AS4809) is essentially a premium backbone that routes traffic with lower latency and fewer hops into mainland China compared to regular transit routes. For users who need stable, reasonably fast connections between US servers and Chinese users, CN2 GIA is usually the go-to. HostDare bundles this with China Unicom (AS9929) and CMIN2 (AS58807) for a triple-network optimization setup on their China-optimized plans.

Beyond the network angle, HostDare runs on KVM virtualization across all plans, supports full root SSH access, and accepts PayPal, credit/debit cards, Alipay, UnionPay, and even cryptocurrency. The three-day money-back guarantee is limited but exists.

Their support is generally responsive, though "small team" response times apply — most users report issues getting resolved within 24 hours.
